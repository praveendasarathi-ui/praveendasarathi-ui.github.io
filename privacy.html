Privacy Policy

Vedic Shorts Agent · Effective 21 November 2026

Vedic Shorts Agent ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Praveen Dasarathi. It creates short educational videos about Hindu scripture and publishes them to two YouTube channels owned by the operator: Vedic Vibes and Sanatan Saaransh. The app is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the operator signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube API Services.

What the app accesses

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): used only to upload the app's own videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the operator's two channels.
  • Read-only access (youtube.readonly): used only to read view, like and comment counts of videos the app itself uploaded, so it can choose better topics.

The app does not read, collect, store or share any information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

What is stored and where

  • An OAuth refresh token for each of the operator's channels, stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in a private repository.
  • A log of the app's own uploads (video ID, title, topic, date and public view counts), stored in that same private repository.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app sets no cookies and this website collects no personal data.

Retention and deletion

Upload logs are kept while the channels are active. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, which immediately stops the app from using YouTube. The stored tokens are deleted when the app is retired.
